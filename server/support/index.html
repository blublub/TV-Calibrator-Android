TV Calibrator

Support

TV Calibrator uses your iPhone's camera as a meter to calibrate your TV's picture, with free test-pattern videos you play on the TV. It's made by Slidebean and is open source.

Get help
Email calibrator@slidebean.com, or report a bug or a wrong menu direction on GitHub. Tell us your iPhone model and your TV's brand and model.

Common questions

Where are the test patterns?

SDR and HDR10 patterns are in the TV Calibrator playlists on the Slidebean YouTube channel. Play them in your TV's own YouTube app. HDR10+ and Dolby Vision patterns are a download, linked in the app, because YouTube doesn't support those formats.

Do I need a colorimeter or a Mac?

No. Your iPhone is the meter. Each supported iPhone model has been matched to a professional colorimeter in advance.

My iPhone says its readings are approximate.

Your iPhone model doesn't have its own camera profile yet, so the app uses the standard profile. Calibration still works, but the numbers are less exact. New profiles are added without an app update.

The app didn't pick up the video.

Start the video from the beginning, let any ad finish, and keep the phone a hand's length (15–30 cm) from the screen, pointed at its centre, in a dark room. Make sure the video matches the mode you picked (SDR or HDR10).

My TV's menus don't match the app.

Menus change between models and firmware versions. Please tell us your TV model and what the menu says, by email or on GitHub, and we'll update the directions.

Can I try it without a TV?

Yes. On the home screen, tap the demo. It simulates a TV and gives you on-screen buttons in place of the remote.
